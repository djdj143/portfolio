FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about websites, automation, apps, pricing and working with Doman Sahu. Still unsure? WhatsApp me.

How fast do you deliver?add
Most business websites take 1–2 weeks, depending on scope. Business automation projects typically take 3–7 days, while custom web applications and mobile apps may take 3–8 weeks. You will receive an estimated timeline before development starts.
How much does a website cost?add
Website development starts from ₹8,000 for a starter website. Business websites start from ₹15,000, while custom web applications start from ₹30,000+. The final quotation depends on your requirements and features.
Do you offer payment in installments?add
Yes. For most projects, payment can be structured around project milestones, such as an initial advance and the remaining amount after agreed development milestones or delivery.
What happens after my website is launched?add
You will receive the completed website and deployment support. Post-launch support and maintenance can also be provided depending on your requirements.
Do you work with clients outside Raipur?add
Absolutely. I work with businesses across India. Projects can be managed remotely through WhatsApp, email, Google Meet, Zoom and other online communication.
What services do you provide?add
My main services include website development, business automation, MIS and dashboards, custom web applications, ERP/business software, Android/mobile applications, API integrations, AI-powered solutions, and WhatsApp/email automation.
Do you build websites with admin panels?add
Yes. Depending on the project, I can develop websites with admin panels/CMS functionality so you can manage content, products, enquiries, users or business data without changing the website code.
What is business automation?add
Business automation uses software, APIs, Google Sheets, databases, WhatsApp, email and AI to reduce repetitive manual work. For example: Customer Enquiry → Automatic Notification → Google Sheet → Follow-up → Dashboard/Report.
Can you automate my existing business process?add
Yes. You do not necessarily need to replace your entire system. I can understand your existing workflow first and identify which parts can be automated or improved.
Do you provide WhatsApp automation?add
Yes. WhatsApp can be integrated into business workflows for lead capture, notifications, customer communication and follow-ups. The exact setup and third-party/API charges depend on the requirement.
Can you connect my website with Google Sheets?add
Yes. Websites and web applications can be integrated with Google Sheets for lead collection, enquiry management, reports and other business workflows where appropriate.
Do you develop Android and mobile apps?add
Yes. Android/mobile applications can be developed according to your business requirements. Cost and timeline depend on features, backend requirements and integrations.
Can you develop ERP or business software?add
Yes. Custom ERP/business software can be developed for processes such as inventory, sales, purchase, customer management, reporting, staff workflows and dashboards. These projects are quoted after understanding the requirements.
Do you provide maintenance after development?add
Yes. Ongoing maintenance and support can be arranged for websites, applications and automation systems, including bug fixes, updates, monitoring and feature changes depending on the support plan.
I don't know exactly what I need. Can you help?add
Absolutely. You don't need a technical specification to get started. Tell me about your business and the problem you are trying to solve. I'll suggest a practical solution and explain the expected cost and timeline.
How do I get started?add
Click Get Quote or Start Your Project, fill in the enquiry form, and share your requirement. I'll review it and get back to you to discuss the next steps.
